Practical Auto Maintenance Tips and Customer Answers

Taking care of your vehicle does not have to be complicated. Below, our service advisors have compiled answers to our most common customer questions along with practical auto maintenance tips to help you prevent unexpected roadside breakdowns.

How often should I schedule an oil and filter change?

Following manufacturer-recommended intervals is one of the best auto maintenance tips for protecting your engine. Most modern vehicles running full synthetic oil require service every 5,000 to 7,500 miles, while older models using conventional oil should be serviced every 3,000 to 5,000 miles.

Where can I get immediate check engine light help?

If your dashboard warning indicator illuminates, visit our shop right away for professional check engine light help. A steady light usually signals an emissions or sensor issue, such as a loose gas cap or failing oxygen sensor. However, if the light is flashing, pull over safely and turn off the engine immediately to prevent severe catalytic converter damage.

Quick Preventive Checklist for Drivers

  • Check tire air pressure monthly to improve fuel efficiency and tread life
  • Inspect windshield wiper blades every six months for streaking or cracking
  • Listen for high-pitched squealing noises when braking, which indicate worn brake pads

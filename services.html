Comprehensive Car Brake Repair and Preventive Maintenance

At Apex Auto Care, we offer a complete range of mechanical services designed to protect your investment and keep your vehicle operating at peak performance. From quick routine fluid services to complete car brake repair, our technicians follow strict manufacturer specifications on every vehicle we service.

Routine Maintenance and Synthetic Oil Change Packages

Consistent preventive care is the easiest way to avoid costly breakdowns down the road. Every synthetic oil change includes a new OEM-grade oil filter, chassis lubrication, and a thorough visual safety inspection.

  • Full synthetic, synthetic blend, and conventional synthetic oil change services
  • Tire rotation, wheel balancing, tread depth checks, and precision four-wheel alignments
  • Complete fluid exchanges for engine coolant, transmission, brake lines, and power steering
  • Engine air filter and cabin air filter replacements for cleaner airflow

Engine Diagnostic Service and Mechanical Repairs

When your vehicle experiences rough idling, squealing brakes, or reduced fuel economy, our advanced engine diagnostic service quickly identifies the problem.

  • Complete car brake repair including ceramic brake pads, rotors, calipers, and ABS sensors
  • Computerized engine diagnostic service for check engine lights and emissions failures
  • Battery load testing, alternator replacement, and starter motor repairs
  • Suspension and steering repairs including shocks, struts, ball joints, and tie rods
